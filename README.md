## What's new
- Dropdown place pickers (list narrows to places connected by a bus)
- 8 demo buses on South Goa routes with fake plates, bays and service hours
- Official fares (student/adult, half/full, +₹5 electric AC) and a "last bus" late-night alert
- Alternative transport with fair-fare ranges, a quoted-fare checker, and a walk tip for short trips
- Report a problem (passenger) feeding a Complaints page in Admin
- Safe waiting tips, "Share my trip" and "alert me at 2 min"
- Language switcher: English, Konkani, Hindi, Kannada (demo translations)
